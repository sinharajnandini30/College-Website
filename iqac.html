About IQAC
Established in 2013 under the guidance and regulations of the University Grants Commission (UGC), the IQAC at Ranchi University is dedicated to
fostering a culture of continuous spimprovement, academic excellence, and quality assurance.
Our commitment to delivering high-quality education aligns with global standards,
ensuring a dynamic and enriching learning environment for our students.

Vision
striving for Continuous Improvement and Excellence in Higher Education. Our vision at IQAC is to be a catalyst for the continuous enhancement of academic and administrative processes,
fostering a culture of quality consciousness and innovation. We aim to create an environment that encourages
creativity, critical thinking, and holistic development, ensuring that our institution
stands as a beacon of quality education.

Mission
Empowering Excellence through Robust Quality Assurance Mechanisms Our mission is to develop and implement robust quality assurance mechanisms that contribute to the overall
improvement of the institution. We are dedicated to:

1. Ensuring Academic Excellence: Through regular reviews, assessments, and feedback mechanisms, we aim to elevate the quality of academic programs, teaching, and research.

2. Enhancing Administrative Processes: We strive to optimize administrative processes, ensuring efficiency, transparency, and responsiveness to the evolving needs of the academic community.

3. Promoting Research and Innovation: By fostering a culture of research and innovation, we aim to contribute to the body of knowledge and equip our students and faculty with the skills and mindset needed for the future.

4. Nurturing a Culture of Continuous Improvement: Our commitment to continuous improvement involves regular self-assessment, benchmarking against best practices, and implementing corrective measures.

The IQAC is dedicated to creating a vibrant and dynamic learning environment where every stakeholder – students, faculty, staff, and the community – actively contributes to the pursuit of excellence. We believe that a quality-driven education empowers individuals to make meaningful contributions to society.
