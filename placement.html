Placement Office

 There is a well organised placement cell in Ranchi Women's College,Ranchi, which regularly invites different Industrial and Corporate
Houses from all over India for Campus Recruitment drive for Final Year Students.
The Placement Cell of the college is working under the chairmanship of principal Dr. Manoj Kumar and headed by Dr. R.R.Sharma Sir followed by a list of team members who coordinate along with the visiting team (24*7) for the support & success of a placement drive.
Companies like SAP, L&T, TCS Ignite, WIPRO TECHNOLOGIES, TCS IT Services, Concentrix, TCS BPS, Airtel , ICICI & many more have undertaken campus drives & selected students across all departments through online tests & series of technical & HR rounds in accordance to their merit .
